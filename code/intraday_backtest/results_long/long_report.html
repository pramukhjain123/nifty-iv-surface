How close is PNmix to the market?

Median distance between the traded price and PNmix fair value: ₹0.057 (0.024 vol points). Pricing each strike from its two neighbours instead: ₹0.090.

How it was run

  • Every minute: last traded price of every NIFTY option within about ±6% of spot (candles with volume, at most 2 minutes old), forward from put-call parity, nearest two expiries with 2+ hours left, PNmix fit. Same pipeline as PNmix Live.
  • Signal: the gap beats STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp duty and GST at the modelled bid/ask; for 10 lots it must also cover the ₹47.20 brokerage (₹4.72 per lot).
  • "Wait 1 min": filled at the next minute's opening trade ± half-spread. "No wait": filled at the price that showed the gap ± half-spread, which needs an automated order within seconds; 10-lot runs also need 10+ lots traded in the next minute.
  • Exit when the mid crosses the current fair value, after 60 minutes, at 15:25, or after 10 minutes without trades; exit pays the spread again.
  • Spread model (no historical bid/ask in candles): median half-spread by price level from a real Upstox option-chain snapshot; tested at 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× and with 1–2 extra ticks per fill.
  • The 90% range and chance of a losing total come from resampling whole days 5,000 times, because trades on the same day are related.

Limits

  • Every 4th trading day only (about 125 of about 495 sessions); results scale roughly 4× for all days. Lot size changed 25→75→65 over the period and per-lot figures use the lot in force; STT and other charges use the October 2026 rates throughout. The spread model comes from one October 2026 snapshot.
  • Last-traded prices bounce between bid and ask, which flatters "no wait" results. The live track record, which uses real bid/ask quotes, is the better test.
  • Order-book depth for 10 lots is not known; the slippage rows show how fast the result shrinks.
